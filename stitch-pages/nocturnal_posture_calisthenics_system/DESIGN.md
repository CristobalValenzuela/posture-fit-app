## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for low-light, high-focus evening routines—specifically tailored to decompression, structural spine health, and rigorous bodyweight pull-up progressions before sleep. The brand personality is disciplined, restorative, silent, and technically precise. It bridges physical therapy rigor with athletic mastery, avoiding the frenetic neon aesthetics of standard workout trackers in favor of an intentional, nocturnal sanctuary.

The core visual style merges **Nocturnal Minimalism** with **Tactile OLED Functionalism**. It avoids visual noise, glare, and hyperactive micro-animations that disrupt melatonin production. High optical contrast, disciplined negative space, and clear glanceability allow users to operate the PWA mid-set or while hanging from a bar with a single hand in a dim bedroom or garage gym.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for OLED black fidelity, dark-adapted vision, and rapid biometric feedback:

- **Canvas & Background (`#0F172A`)**: Deepest slate. Anchors the view with zero glare, minimizing eye strain during nighttime workouts.
- **Surface Elevation (`#1E293B`)**: Card, sheet, and dock base tier. Provides clear spatial separation from the canvas without introducing elevated brightness.
- **Structural Outlines (`#334155`)**: Slate border tone for crisp boundaries, tactile card perimeters, and modular dividers.
- **Primary Accent (`#10B981`)**: Soft, luminous emerald green. Signifies set completions, active hold confirmations, streak continuity, and posture integrity scores.
- **Secondary Accent (`#F59E0B`)**: Warm amber. Reserved exclusively for temporal states—active rest countdowns, interval transitions, and deliberate hang holds.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#F43F5E`)**: Muted coral rose. Communicates missed calendar targets, form degradation alerts, and active rep fatigue limits without aggressive, high-alarm saturation.
- **Typography - High Contrast (`#F8FAFC`)**: Crisp slate white for active digits, exercise titles, and primary metrics. Ensures instant glanceability at arm's length.
- **Typography - Low Contrast (`#94A3B8`)**: Muted slate for anatomical cues, secondary labels, set metadata, and completed sub-tasks.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes functional legibility under muscular exertion and arm's-length viewing. 

- **Primary Pairing**: **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides geometric presence and structural clarity for large-scale counters, set numbers, and titles. **Inter** powers technical cues, anatomical annotations, and form checklists where optical neutrality and clean micro-legibility are paramount.
- **Tabular Numerals**: Timers, rep counters, and rest interval clocks must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum"` to prevent horizontal jitter during down-counts.
- **Hierarchy Rules**: Workout headers rely on `headline-lg` in `#F8FAFC`, while exercise details and set instructions sit within `body-md` rendered in `#94A3B8`. Micro-badges and set tags use uppercase `label-sm` with widened letter-spacing to remain legible on deep surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first around a single-column, thumb-accessible stack designed for one-handed operation. 

- **PWA Constraints**: Maximum content container width is locked at 480px on larger screens, centering the interface like an integrated console.
- **Vertical Reach Zones**: Critical actions (rep confirmation, timer pause, set completion) sit strictly within the bottom 35% of the viewport (the primary thumb zone). Visual metrics (posture diagrams, streak arcs, timers) occupy the upper 65%.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Based strictly on a 4px/8px incremental grid. Standard card interior padding is `space-lg` (1.5rem), gap between sequential routine blocks is `space-md` (1rem), and list item margins use `space-sm` (0.5rem) to maintain compact, non-scrolling vertical footprints during active routines.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid nighttime glare and unnatural floating planes, this design system discards heavy dropshadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** accompanied by **Subtle Border Contours**:

- **Ground Level (Base Viewport)**: Solid `#0F172A`. Deep, static canvas.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surfaces)**: Solid `#1E293B` encased with a 1px solid border of `#334155`. Cards do not use drop shadows; depth is strictly tactile and surface-differentiated.
- **Level 2 (Active/Floating Overlays & Sheets)**: `#1E293B` with a secondary border stroke of `#334155`, backed by a subtle 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6) ambient shadow. Used exclusively for bottom sheet drawers, timer popovers, and sticky lower navigation pills.
- **Interactive State Elevation**: Tapping or holding an element produces an instant boundary tint shift from `#334155` to the active state token (`#10B981` or `#F59E0B`), rather than upward physical translation.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on **Generous Curvature (`roundedness: 2`)** coupled with specialized full-pill terminals for touch execution points:

- **Cards and Structural Surfaces**: Hard-coded to 16px corner radius (`rounded-2xl` equivalent) to soften the perimeter and establish clean containment for technical diagrams and metrics.
- **Primary Interactive Targets**: Exercise check-ins, main action triggers, and navigation selectors use full-radius pill silhouettes (9999px / `rounded-full`) to offer an unmistakable, ergonomic touch profile.
- **Status Indicators and Badges**: 8px (`rounded-md`) to 12px (`rounded-lg`) corner radii keep small indicators visually harmonious without competing with the pill buttons.

## Components

### Buttons & Touch Targets
- **Primary Action (Pill)**: Full pill radius, minimum height of 56px to guarantee single-hand hit accuracy while fatigued. Background: `#10B981` with high-contrast text (`#0F172A`, weight 700). Active touch state dims to 90% opacity with no layout shift.
- **Rest/Hold Action (Pill)**: Minimum height 56px. Background: `#F59E0B` with dark slate text (`#0F172A`).
- **Secondary/Ghost Buttons**: Full pill radius, minimum height 48px. Background: transparent; 1.5px border of `#334155`; label text `#F8FAFC`. Active state triggers background `#1E293B`.

### Workout & Posture Cards
- **Architecture**: Container surface `#1E293B`, 1px solid border `#334155`, 16px border-radius, `space-lg` interior padding.
- **States**: 
  - *Active/Current Set*: Border transitions to `#10B981` (1.5px thickness).
  - *Completed*: Subtle opacity reduction of content text to 70%, with a crisp `#10B981` badge.
  - *Missed/Skipped*: Border accent shifts to `#F43F5E` (1px).

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Used for workout tagging (e.g., "Thoracic Extension", "Weighted Pull-Up", "Dead Hang").
- Minimum height 32px, full pill shape, surface `#1E293B`, border 1px `#334155`, text `label-md` in `#94A3B8`. Selected state fills the chip with `#334155` and text `#F8FAFC`.

### Form Checklists & Set Selectors
- Custom nocturnal checkboxes: 24x24px rounded rectangles (6px radius), 1.5px border of `#334155`. When checked, surfaces fill with `#10B981` with an interior crisp white `#0F172A` checkmark.
- Tap area is extended to a 48px touch zone around the target.

### Rest Interval & Hang Clocks
- Displayed prominently inside cards or sticky bottom sheets using `timer-display` typography.
- Surrounded by a circular SVG stroke rail in `#334155` with an animated countdown ring in `#F59E0B` (for rest) or `#10B981` (for active isometric hold).

### Daily Streak Calendar Matrix
- Micro-square matrix (7 columns for days of week).
- Uncompleted future days: `#1E293B` with border `#334155`.
- Successfully decompressed/trained days: Solid `#10B981`.
- Missed designated routine days: Solid `#F43F5E`.
- Neutral rest days: Border `#334155` with an interior 2px slate dot.
